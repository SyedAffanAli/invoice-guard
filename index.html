Why now

The UAE is changing how every B2B invoice works, on a fixed deadline.

Ministerial Decisions 243 and 244 of 2025 require invoices to travel as structured data through accredited providers on the Peppol network.

1 Jan 2026

Stricter UAE VAT rules: a claim can be refused if the buyer should have known.

30 Oct 2026

Large firms must appoint an accredited e-invoicing provider.

1 Jan 2027

E-invoicing goes live for companies with AED 50m+ revenue.

1 Jul 2027

All other businesses go live.

E-invoicing checks that a bill is correctly formatted. It does not check that the bill is correct.

Questions

What people ask first.

Do we have to replace our ERP or e-invoicing provider?

No. Invoice Guard sits next to them. Your ERP still records and pays bills, and your provider still sends and receives e-invoices.

Is this tax advice?

No. It checks bills against official rules and flags problems. Grey areas such as blocked VAT are marked "needs review" for your tax adviser.

Where is our data kept?

On Azure in the UAE, encrypted, with each company's data separated and every check logged. E-invoice data must stay in the UAE.

What if the rules or dates change again?

Rules and dates are settings, not hard-coded, and updates are tracked against Ministry of Finance announcements.

How long until we see results?

A free sample review on a few months of bills shows what a first scan finds, usually within days of receiving the data.
